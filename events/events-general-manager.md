### Case 1: Large-Scale Conference P&L Recovery Post-Pandemic
Scenario: when you take over a flagship 5,000-attendee annual industry conference with revenue trending 40% below pre-pandemic levels and exhibitor renewal rate at only 34%, you must rebuild profitability within 2 event cycles. Diagnosis: attendee survey data via Cvent reveals the previous organizer raised registration fees by 25% to cover fixed venue costs (cancellation penalty from the convention center contract) while cutting content quality — resulting in NPS dropping from +42 to -8. Exhibitors cite poor lead retrieval ROI and declining foot traffic to the expo hall. Solution: renegotiate the venue contract with a reduced minimum guarantee in exchange for a 3-year commitment (spreading risk), redesign the floor plan using AllSeated or Social Tables to create "expo theaters" on the show floor (free content stages that keep attendees in the exhibit hall), introduce AI-powered matchmaking via Grip or Brella for 1:1 buyer-seller meetings scheduled pre-event, and implement dynamic pricing for registration (early bird $699 → late $1,099). For exhibitors, offer lead retrieval analytics dashboards via Swapcard comparing booth traffic against category benchmarks. Result: attendance recovered to 4,200 with NPS of +38, exhibitor renewal rate increased to 62%, event EBITDA margin improved from 8% to 22%.

### Case 2: Virtual/Hybrid Event Pivot
Scenario: you're launching a new B2B summit series where 60% of registered attendees are international and visa/travel constraints limit physical attendance to 40% of registrations. You must design a hybrid experience that delivers equal value to virtual and in-person participants without cannibalizing physical ticket sales. Diagnosis: the initial plan priced virtual access at $199 and physical access at $899 — early data shows 73% of registrations choosing virtual, threatening the physical event's financial viability and exhibitor ROI. Solution: implement a tiered hybrid model: "Virtual Pass" ($299, access to all livestreamed keynotes and on-demand sessions via Hopin or Bizzabo), "Physical Pass" ($799, includes everything in Virtual plus in-person networking dinner, roundtables, and expo floor), and "VIP All-Access" ($1,499, includes 6 pre-scheduled 1:1 meetings via Deal Room matchmaking and post-event lead list). Invest in broadcast-quality production — multi-camera setup with Tricaster switching, Sennheiser wireless audio, and dedicated virtual Q&A moderator monitoring Slido. For exhibitors, create "virtual booths" on the event platform with embedded demo videos, downloadable collateral, and live chat staffing during event hours. Result: blended revenue exceeded physical-only projections by 28% (virtual contributed 35% of total revenue), virtual attendees reported 92% satisfaction rate, 18% of virtual attendees upgraded to physical for the next edition.

### Case 3: Vendor Consolidation for Multi-Event Portfolio
Scenario: you're managing a portfolio of 12 annual events (conferences, trade shows, galas) across 4 cities and currently working with 47 different vendors (AV, catering, decor, transportation, security) — vendor management overhead consumes 30% of your operations team's time. Diagnosis: spend analysis using Coupa or Procurify reveals the top 10 vendors (20% of vendor count) account for 78% of total spend but are managed through 47 separate contracts with inconsistent terms, insurance verification, and payment schedules. The fragmented vendor base also means zero volume-based negotiation leverage. Solution: implement a Strategic Vendor Partner program — consolidate into 5 preferred vendor categories (AV partner: Encore/PSAV for consistency across venues, catering: local culinary partners per city with standardized menus vetted for HACCP compliance, decor: single national design firm with regional warehousing, security: national provider with event-specific staffing plans, transportation: rideshare partnership with Uber for Business plus dedicated shuttle operator). Negotiate master service agreements (MSAs) with annual volume commitments in exchange for 12-18% rate reduction and COI (Certificate of Insurance) auto-renewal via vendor management platform. Standardize the RFP process using Aventri templates with scoring rubrics for quality/cost/reliability/sustainability. Result: vendor count reduced from 47 to 11, vendor-related operations workload decreased by 55%, total vendor spend reduced by 14% through volume consolidation, event-day vendor no-show incidents dropped to zero (from 3 per year average).
